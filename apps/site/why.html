Why JustAsk

Help should arrive the moment it’s needed.

Mission

Seamless access to everyday technology should not depend on how old you are, or on whether family is in the room. As AI becomes part of daily life, younger people absorb the next interface. Older adults are left waiting.

JustAsk is there the moment they need help, including when family is away and there is no class, no IT desk, and no video to watch first.

Vision

An older adult, especially in a community where family is not nearby, can finish the task in front of her when it matters. She says what she needs. JustAsk shows the next step in a way she can see and hear, or it does the safe step for her.

She stops having to hand every immediate task to someone else.

Who it’s for

Someone who is capable of the task but stuck on the next click, at the moment it is urgent: when the family member who usually helps is at work, in another city, or asleep in another time zone.

The next click is urgent

The same hour can bring any of these.

  • Signing up for, or signing in to, a patient portal
  • Joining a video visit with a doctor
  • Reading a test result
  • Refilling a prescription
  • Making the email account a benefits site requires
  • Filling out a form, or uploading a document
  • A browser video call with family on Zoom or Meet
  • Telling whether a page is a scam

What she can ask

Plain words, on the site she already has open.

  • “Help me sign in.”
  • “Help me refill my prescription.”
  • “Help me join the video visit.”
  • “Help me send this email.”
  • “Help me fill out this form.”

What the AI actually is

One step, then look again.

Claude’s one-step loop

Claude (claude-opus-5-5) sees a numbered snapshot of the current page and returns one next step. JustAsk highlights it, speaks it, and either guides her or does that one safe action. It checks that the page changed, then plans again. It isn’t an open-ended agent, and it never leaves the browser.

The page scanner

Turns what’s on screen into numbered controls Claude can point at. Snapshots are not stored.

Cartesia voice

Speech-to-text and text-to-speech in the extension. Convex mints a short-lived token, so the Cartesia key stays on the server. Browser speech remains a setting if Cartesia is unavailable.

Convex for the helper

Powers the caregiver side: sign-in, the live pairing code, tasks, and the Connected pill that updates without a refresh.

Safety gates

  • Passwords, codes, and card numbers are never typed by JustAsk.
  • Money, account changes, and personal information ask first.
  • A page that looks like a scam is blocked.
  • Repeat and Stop are always on the surface.
  • Snapshots of the page are not stored.
