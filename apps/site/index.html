A Chrome extension and a caregiver site

Seamless access to everyday technology shouldn’t depend on your age.

Or on whether family is in the room. JustAsk is there the moment she needs help, on the website she already has open: no class, no IT desk, no video to watch first.

How it helps

One step at a time, on the page in front of her.

1 · Ask

She says what she needs.

One button, then plain words: “Help me refill my prescription.”

2 · See and hear

The next step lights up.

A glowing box marks the next control, and JustAsk speaks one short line.

3 · Do

She clicks, or JustAsk does the safe step.

A safe step is a click, a search, or a field that isn’t sensitive. Then it checks that the page changed and plans the next one.
