The lesson

Use the extension and the caregiver app together.

Two people, two computers, one task. Follow these five steps in order.

  1. The helper

    Open the caregiver app and create her profile.

    Sign in at the caregiver app, add the person you help, and read the 6-digit code it shows.

    Open the caregiver app

  2. On her computer

    Load the extension, turn it on, and enter the code.

    Load JustAsk into Chrome (the steps are here), press Turn JustAsk on, and type the helper’s code in Settings.

  3. The helper

    Send a task.

    For example: “Refill a prescription.”

  4. On her computer

    Open JustAsk and press Start.

    The glowing box and the spoken line take it from there, one step at a time.

  5. The helper

    Watch it change: Connected, then Done.

    The caregiver app updates live, without refreshing.

Where this website stops

This site’s job ends at the links.

Everything after happens in the real apps. Chrome setup, the side panel, scanning, and planning live in the extension. Sign-in, the 6-digit code, tasks, and the live Connected pill live in the caregiver app, talking to Convex. This page never sees the page she is on, the code, or the task.
